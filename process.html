Process

A clear process. You stay in control.

You share your website and vision first. We agree the scope and quote before you pay a deposit or commit to the project.

01

Share your current website and vision

Tell us what you want to improve and share the style, images and features you have in mind.

02

Agree the scope and quote

We review what you need and confirm the work, price and included revision round before starting.

03

Pay the €45 deposit to begin

Payment is arranged privately after we agree to work together. There is no payment form or checkout on this website.

04

Review the private preview

We build the site on a temporary link. You review it and send the included round of changes.

Further rounds or additional work are priced and agreed before they begin.
05

Approve the preview, then pay the €105 balance

The €105 is the remaining balance. It is due after your approval and before we publish the site to your domain.

06

Launch and hand off

You provide secure access to your domain and hosting. We connect the site, explain its features and editing, and share the code on GitHub.

After handoff, you manage the site. Ongoing maintenance is not included.
