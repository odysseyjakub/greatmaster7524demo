Good to know

Clear answers, before we begin.

We’ll confirm your exact scope and quote before the deposit, so you know what to expect.

Do I have to pay through this website?+

No. There is no checkout here. If we agree to work together, we’ll arrange payment privately using a method that works for you.

What happens before I pay the deposit?+

You share your current website and your ideas. We discuss what you need and agree the project scope, price and included revision round first.

Can I see the website before it goes live?+

Yes. You receive a private preview on a temporary link. The site moves to your domain after you approve it and pay the remaining €105.

Who owns my domain and hosting?+

You do. You purchase and pay for them directly, then provide access securely when it is time to connect the finished site. Never send passwords through a public message.

Do you maintain the site after launch?+

No. The project ends at handoff. We explain the site's features and editing, and provide the open-source code so it can be changed in future.

What if I want more changes?+

One revision round is included in the agreed project. If you want extra rounds or work beyond the agreed scope, we’ll confirm the additional price before doing it.
