Services

A complete website refresh, built around your brief.

We work from your current website and the direction you provide. Share the style, images and features you want, and we’ll agree the project scope with you before we begin.

01 · Redesign

Your existing website, renewed

We reshape your current site into a clearer, more modern experience for your customers.

02 · Your direction

Style chosen by you

Your preferred mood, colours, images and ideas guide the look and feel of the finished site.

03 · Responsive

Designed for every screen

A considered layout for phones, tablets and desktop computers.

04 · Contact

Useful contact features

We include the contact features agreed for your project, such as email, phone or an enquiry form.

05 · Launch

Private preview, then your domain

Review the site on a temporary link. After approval and final payment, we connect it to your domain.

06 · Handoff

Code you can keep

We explain the site's features and editing, and provide its source code on GitHub for future changes.

This is a website redesign and launch service. It does not include online purchases or ongoing website maintenance. Your domain and hosting remain yours.
