FAQs

A few things to know.

We’ll answer your project-specific questions and confirm the scope before any deposit is due.

Do I pay through this website?+

No. There is no checkout here. If we agree to work together, payment is arranged privately using a method that works for both of us.

What do I share before getting a quote?+

Send your current website and describe what you want to change. You can also share your preferred style, images and features.

Can I see the website before it goes live?+

Yes. You receive a private preview on a temporary link. After approval, the €105 remaining balance is due before we publish the site to your domain.

How many changes are included?+

One revision round is included in the agreed project. We price any further rounds or additional work with you before proceeding.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?+

You own and pay for your domain and hosting. For launch, provide access using a secure invite or account permission rather than sending a password in a public message.

Do you maintain the site after launch?+

No. The project ends with launch and handoff. We explain the site's features and editing, and share its open-source code on GitHub for future changes.

Do you show demos or portfolio examples?+

We don’t publish demos or portfolio examples by default. Your own site is shared through a private preview while we work on your project.
